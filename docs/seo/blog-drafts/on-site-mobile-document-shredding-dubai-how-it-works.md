## AEO Answer Block

**How does on-site document shredding work in Dubai?**
On-site shredding means the destruction happens at your premises rather than documents being taken away first. For a rented office shredder, this is continuous — your team feeds documents through as needed, with the machine on-site the whole rental period. For a mobile shredding service, a truck-mounted industrial shredder arrives, destroys your prepared documents on the spot (often while you watch), and leaves with only shredded material. Both remove the security gap of documents travelling off-site intact.

## Two Different Things Called "On-Site Shredding"

### 1. A Rented Shredder, Kept On-Site

You rent a shredder (from AED 150/month), it's delivered and set up at your office, and your team shreds documents whenever needed for the length of the contract. This is on-site in the truest sense — the machine never leaves your premises, and nothing waits to be collected.

### 2. A Mobile Shredding Truck

A shredding company sends a truck with an industrial shredder mounted inside. You bring your documents out (or they're already boxed), the truck shreds them on the spot, and the confetti/pulp leaves in the truck — no intact documents are ever transported off-site. This suits one-off, high-volume clear-outs where a rented office unit isn't sized for the job.

---

## What Happens on the Day (Mobile Shredding)

1. **Booking** — confirm volume (boxes/bins) and access requirements for the truck at your building or free zone.
2. **Arrival** — the truck parks at your premises; most UAE providers can access standard office parking and loading bays.
3. **Destruction** — documents are fed into the on-board shredder. Many services let you watch via a viewing window or camera feed.
4. **Certificate** — you receive a certificate of destruction confirming date, volume, and method.
5. **Departure** — only shredded material leaves; nothing readable exits the site.

## FAQ

**Q: Is on-site shredding safer than sending documents away?**
A: Yes — it removes the transport window where intact documents could be lost, misdelivered, or accessed. Both a rented on-site shredder and a mobile shredding truck avoid that risk; only "drop-off" or "mail-in" shredding services carry it.

**Q: Can a mobile shredding truck access free zones like JAFZA or DIFC?**
A: Most can, subject to the free zone's own vehicle access rules — confirm with your provider ahead of time. Sahara delivers rental shredders to JAFZA, SAIF Zone, DAFZA, and DIFC as standard; see [free zone delivery](/blogs/shredder-delivery-jafza-saif-zone-difc-uae-free-zones/).

**Q: What volume needs a mobile truck instead of a rented office shredder?**
A: If you're clearing out years of archived records in one go — multiple boxes or bins — a mobile truck or a heavy-duty rental unit handles the volume faster than a standard office model. See [heavy-duty shredder rental for bulk clear-outs](/blogs/heavy-duty-shredder-rental-bulk-document-clearout-uae/).

**Q: Do I get proof the documents were destroyed?**
A: Yes, both options can provide a certificate of destruction on request.
