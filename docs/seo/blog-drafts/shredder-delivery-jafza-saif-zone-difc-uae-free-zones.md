## Introduction

Free zone companies in the UAE often assume facilities-style services like equipment rental are harder to arrange than for mainland businesses — extra approvals, restricted delivery access, longer lead times. For paper shredder rental, that's generally not the case: delivery and setup work the same way across free zones as anywhere else in the emirate.

This guide covers what to expect zone by zone.

---

## JAFZA (Jebel Ali Free Zone) — Dubai

JAFZA is one of the largest free zones in the region, covering logistics, trading, and industrial tenants. Shredder rental delivery to JAFZA follows standard Dubai lead times; larger industrial tenants often need departmental-capacity models (see the [Fellowes 325Ci](/services/paper-shredder-rental/)) given higher document volumes from trade and customs paperwork.

## DAFZA (Dubai Airport Free Zone)

DAFZA tenants — aviation, logistics, and trading companies — typically need consistent, compliant document destruction given the volume of shipping and customs paperwork. Same-day and next-day delivery is available depending on order time.

## SAIF Zone (Sharjah Airport International Free Zone)

SAIF Zone is close to Sahara's Sharjah headquarters, meaning some of the fastest delivery times in the network — often same-day for orders placed early.

## DIFC (Dubai International Financial Centre)

DIFC hosts financial services firms, law firms, and professional services companies — sectors with above-average document sensitivity (client files, contracts, financial records). DIFC also operates its own data protection regime (DIFC Data Protection Law) layered on top of UAE federal PDPL, making secure document destruction doubly relevant for DIFC-registered entities.

## Abu Dhabi Free Zones (including ADGM)

Abu Dhabi free zone tenants, including ADGM-registered entities (which — like DIFC — operate an additional data protection framework), receive delivery on standard Abu Dhabi lead times.
