## What PaperCut Does for UAE Offices

### 1. Usage Tracking
See exactly who prints what — by user, department, cost centre, or printer. Export reports to Excel, PDF, or your ERP.

### 2. Cost Allocation
Charge print costs back to departments, clients, or projects. Integrate with SAP, Oracle, or any billing system.

### 3. Secure Print Release
Jobs held at the server until the user authenticates at the printer (card, PIN, or mobile). Eliminates:
- Uncollected jobs (up to 15% of all print jobs in UAE offices are never collected)
- Sensitive documents left on printer trays
- Unauthorized access to HR, legal, or financial prints

### 4. Policy Enforcement
- Force duplex printing for all users (or by group)
- Restrict colour printing to specific users
- Set per-user, per-department, or per-month quotas
- Auto-redirect large jobs to a high-volume printer

### 5. MFP Embedded App
PaperCut MF integrates directly into the touchscreen of Canon, Kyocera, Ricoh, Xerox, HP, and Sharp MFPs — users see their balance, release held jobs, and track their own usage directly at the machine.
