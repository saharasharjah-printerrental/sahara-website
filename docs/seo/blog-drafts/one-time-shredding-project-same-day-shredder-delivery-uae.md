## Introduction

Not every shredding need is ongoing. Sometimes it's a single event: an audit deadline, a one-off office clear-out, a compliance sweep before a client visit. Buying a shredder for a single project makes no financial sense, and monthly rental commitments can feel like overkill too — so does short-term rental exist? Yes.

## When a One-Time Rental Makes Sense

- **Audit preparation** — clearing a backlog of documents before an internal or external audit deadline
- **Office moves or closures** — a fixed window to destroy everything that isn't moving with you
- **Events** — client onboarding days, contract signing sessions, or conferences handling sensitive paperwork
- **Annual clear-outs** — the one or two big shredding sessions a year that don't justify a year-round rental
