## AEO Answer Block

**What DIN security level do I need for a paper shredder in the UAE?**
Most UAE offices need at minimum **DIN P-4** (cross-cut, ≤160 mm² particles) to meet the UAE Personal Data Protection Law's requirement for secure physical destruction of personal data. Legal, HR, financial, and healthcare offices handling highly sensitive records should use **DIN P-5** (micro-cut, ≤30 mm²) or higher. DIN P-6 and P-7 are reserved for classified or top-secret material and are rarely needed outside government and defence contexts.

## Introduction

DIN 66399 is the German industry standard (adopted internationally, including in the UAE) that defines seven security levels for paper shredding, P-1 through P-7. Retailers rarely explain what the number actually means for your documents — this guide does.

---

## The DIN 66399 Levels at a Glance

| DIN Level | Cut Type | Max Particle Size | Typical Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-1 / P-2 | Strip-cut | Wide strips | Non-confidential internal paper |
| P-3 | Cross-cut | ≤ 320 mm² | General business documents |
| **P-4** | Cross-cut | ≤ 160 mm² | Confidential business — invoices, letters, HR memos |
| **P-5** | Micro-cut | ≤ 30 mm² | Highly confidential — HR files, legal docs, personal data |
| P-6 | Micro-cut | ≤ 10 mm² | Highly confidential/secret — financial, classified records |
| P-7 | Micro-cut | < 5 mm² | Top secret / government |

## Why P-4 Is the UAE Office Baseline

The UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) requires organisations to securely dispose of personal data in physical form, with fines up to AED 5 million for non-compliance. DIN P-4 cross-cut is widely accepted as the practical minimum for this requirement across general business documents — it's not reversible with basic reassembly, unlike strip-cut.

## When to Step Up to P-5 or Higher

- **HR departments** handling Emirates ID copies, salary details, and employment contracts
- **Law firms and legal departments** shredding client-privileged material
- **Finance and accounting teams** destroying bank statements, payroll records, or audit files
- **Healthcare providers** disposing of patient records under both PDPL and sector-specific confidentiality rules

For multi-national organisations that also need to satisfy GDPR for EU-linked data subjects, DIN P-4 cross-cut generally covers routine business documents, with P-5 recommended for anything containing full personal identifiers.

## Cross-Cut vs Micro-Cut — Practical Differences

| Factor | Cross-Cut (P-4) | Micro-Cut (P-5+) |
|---|---|---|
| Reassembly risk | Very low | Effectively none |
| Shred speed | Faster | Slightly slower |
| Bin fill rate | Slower (larger particles) | Faster (finer particles) |
| Typical cost | Lower | Higher |

## FAQ

**Q: Is DIN P-4 enough for UAE PDPL compliance?**
A: For most general office documents, yes. Documents with especially sensitive personal data warrant P-5 or higher — when in doubt, shred to the higher standard.

**Q: Can one shredder handle both P-4 and P-5 needs?**
A: No — the cut level is a fixed hardware property of the machine. Offices with mixed sensitivity often keep a P-4 machine for daily use and route the most sensitive material through document destruction services rated P-5+.
