## What's Included in the Price (and What Isn't)

**Included as standard:**
- Free delivery and on-site setup across Dubai, Sharjah, and Abu Dhabi
- Servicing, repairs, and blade maintenance for the life of the contract
- Machine swap if a unit underperforms or breaks down
- Collection at the end of the rental term

**What can add to the quote:**
- Free zone delivery (JAFZA, SAIF Zone, DIFC) — usually still free, but confirm before signing
- Same-day/urgent delivery outside standard hours
- Upgrading mid-contract to a higher-capacity model
- Very short (under 1-week) rentals, which are typically priced as a flat job fee rather than a pro-rated monthly rate

There should be **no** separate charge for routine maintenance, jam repairs, or blade servicing — if a quote lists these separately, that's a red flag against the AED 150/month all-inclusive standard.
