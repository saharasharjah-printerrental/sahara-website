## Introduction

An office moving, closing, or clearing out years of archived paperwork faces a different problem than day-to-day shredding: volume. A standard 10-sheet office shredder will take days to work through boxes of old records. This is where heavy-duty and industrial rental comes in.

---

## When You Need Heavy-Duty, Not Standard

Reach for a heavy-duty or industrial unit instead of a standard departmental shredder when:

- You're clearing **multiple years** of archived records in one project
- Volume is measured in **boxes or bins**, not a daily stack
- You're closing or relocating an office and need everything gone within a fixed window
- Your standard rental unit can't keep pace and is overheating or jamming under continuous load

---

## What Heavy-Duty / Industrial Shredder Rental Includes

- **Higher throughput** — 50+ sheets per pass, designed for continuous operation rather than intermittent office use
- **Larger bins** — far less frequent emptying during a bulk job
- **Delivery and collection** built around your clear-out timeline, not an open-ended monthly contract
- **Setup on-site**, so your team can start feeding documents as soon as it arrives

Standard departmental units like the Fellowes Powershred 325Ci (24 sheets/pass, 83L bin) suit ongoing office use; for true bulk clear-outs, ask about heavy-duty and industrial capacity — available on request beyond the standard rental range.

## Planning a Bulk Clear-Out

1. **Estimate volume** — count boxes/bins, not individual pages
2. **Check for special materials** — laminated documents, staples, or binders can affect throughput; flag these when requesting a quote
3. **Confirm timeline** — a rush job over a weekend costs differently than a two-week window
4. **Ask about certificates** — bulk clear-outs, especially of old financial or HR records, often need a destruction certificate for audit purposes
