## Introduction

## Step 1 — Estimate Your Monthly Print Volume

Before you look at any spec sheet, know your volume.

| Office Size | Typical Monthly Volume |
|---|---|
| 1–3 staff | 500–2,000 pages |
| 4–8 staff | 2,000–5,000 pages |
| 9–15 staff | 5,000–10,000 pages |

**How to estimate:** Count the number of sheets used per ream (500) and multiply by how often you replace it. If you go through 2 reams/month per person, a 5-person office prints ~5,000 pages.

---

## Step 2 — Decide A4 vs A3

| Feature | A4 MFP | A3 MFP |
|---|---|---|
| Max paper size | A4 (210 × 297mm) | A3 (297 × 420mm) |
| Best for | Documents, invoices, contracts | Presentations, brochures, drawings |
| Monthly rental (small office) | AED 250–450 | AED 500–900 |
| Physical footprint | Desktop/compact | Freestanding |

**Rule of thumb:** If your office never prints A3, don't pay for it. But if you print 10+ A3 pages/month, an A3 MFP saves you from using an external print shop.

---

## Step 3 — Mono vs Colour

- **Mono (black & white):** Cost per page: AED 0.02–0.04. Best for document-heavy offices (legal, logistics, real estate).
- **Colour:** Cost per page: AED 0.10–0.25 for colour pages. Best for offices with presentations, branding materials, or client-facing outputs.

A colour MFP can print mono pages at the same low cost — so if in doubt, choose colour for future flexibility.

---

## Step 4 — Key Specs to Look For

### Speed (ppm)
- 1–5 staff: 20–25 ppm is sufficient
- 6–15 staff: 30–40 ppm prevents queuing at the printer

### Connectivity
- Wi-Fi: Essential for modern open-plan offices
- Ethernet: More stable for fixed workstations
- Mobile print: Apple AirPrint, Mopria, Google Cloud Print

### Duty Cycle vs Recommended Volume
The duty cycle is the *maximum* — always choose a printer whose *recommended* monthly volume matches your usage. Running a printer at its duty cycle limit every month significantly reduces lifespan.
