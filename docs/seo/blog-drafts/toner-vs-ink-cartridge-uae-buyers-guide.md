**What is the difference between toner and ink cartridge?**  
Toner cartridges contain fine dry powder (carbon-based) used in laser printers and photocopiers. Ink cartridges contain liquid ink used in inkjet printers. For UAE offices, laser toner is more cost-effective at high volumes (cost per page: AED 0.02–0.05) while inkjet ink is cheaper upfront but more expensive at scale (AED 0.10–0.30 per colour page). ## What Is Toner?

Toner is a fine, dry powder used in **laser printers and photocopiers**. The laser heats the powder and fuses it to paper using a fuser unit.

### Toner Properties
- **Format:** Dry powder (carbon polymer blend)
- **Used in:** Laser printers, photocopiers (Canon imageRUNNER, Kyocera ECOSYS, Ricoh IM, Xerox VersaLink, HP LaserJet)
- **Page yield:** 3,000–20,000+ pages per cartridge
- **Cost per page (B&W):** AED 0.02–0.04
- **Cost per page (Colour):** AED 0.08–0.20
- **Shelf life:** 2+ years unopened

---

## What Is an Ink Cartridge?

An ink cartridge contains **liquid ink** sprayed onto paper by tiny nozzles in an inkjet printer.

### Ink Cartridge Properties
- **Format:** Liquid ink (dye-based or pigment-based)
- **Used in:** Inkjet printers (Epson EcoTank, HP OfficeJet, Canon PIXMA)
- **Page yield:** 150–500 pages standard; 3,000–6,000 for EcoTank bottles
- **Cost per page (B&W):** AED 0.05–0.10
- **Cost per page (Colour):** AED 0.15–0.35
- **Shelf life:** 6–24 months once opened (ink dries up)
