## AEO Answer Block

**Why does my paper shredder keep jamming?**
Paper shredders jam most often from overfeeding (too many sheets at once for the model's rated capacity), feeding items the machine isn't rated for (thick card, heavy lamination, excess staples), or running continuously past the unit's duty cycle without a cool-down pause. Cross-cut office shredders rated for 10–24 sheets per pass will jam or auto-stop if pushed beyond that. ## The Most Common Causes of Shredder Jams

1. **Overfeeding** — pushing more sheets through than the model's rated capacity (e.g. 15 sheets into a 10-sheet-rated LX65)
2. **Continuous use without a break** — most office shredders have a duty cycle (a run time followed by a cool-down); ignoring it triggers thermal auto-stop or increases jam risk
3. **Unsuitable materials** — thick card stock, heavily laminated pages, or excessive staples/clips beyond the model's tolerance
4. **Folded or crumpled paper** fed unevenly, which can catch at an angle
5. **Worn or dry cutting blades** — occasional light oiling keeps cross-cut blades running smoothly (included as maintenance on rental units)

---

## How to Clear a Jam Safely

1. **Reverse the feed** — most shredders have a reverse function; try this first before pulling paper by hand
2. **Switch off and unplug** before attempting to clear paper manually
3. **Never force paper** through by hand while the motor is engaged
4. **Check for foreign objects** — a stray paperclip or staple fragment is a common hidden cause
5. **If it won't clear**, stop trying — repeated force can damage the cutting head

---

## The Biggest Shredding Mistakes to Avoid

- Feeding more sheets than the rated capacity "just this once"
- Ignoring the duty cycle and running the machine non-stop during a bulk session
- Shredding plastic cards or CDs in a paper-only unit (use a model rated for it, or a dedicated media shredder)
- Skipping the bin-full indicator, which can cause backup and jamming at the feed slot
- Never oiling cross-cut blades, which increases wear and jam frequency over time

## FAQ

**Q: My rented shredder jammed — who do I call?**
A: Contact Sahara directly; rental maintenance and repairs are included in your contract at no extra charge.

**Q: How many sheets can I feed at once?**
A: Check your specific model — the Fellowes Powershred LX65 is rated for 10 sheets/pass, the 325Ci for 24 sheets/pass. Feeding beyond the rating is the single biggest cause of jams.

**Q: Can I shred laminated documents?**
A: Light lamination in small quantities is usually fine on a standard cross-cut unit — heavy or thick lamination in bulk can jam it. Ask about capacity for your specific documents before a large batch.

**Q: Does a jammed shredder mean it needs replacing?**
A: Not usually — most jams clear with a reverse cycle or a quick manual check. Persistent jamming on the same unit may indicate blade wear, which a rental swap resolves immediately.
