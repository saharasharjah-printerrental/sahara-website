## Introduction

"Should we hire a shredding company, or just get a shredder in the office?" is a question every UAE business with confidential paperwork eventually asks. Both destroy documents securely — the difference is in cost, control, and convenience. Here's how to decide.

---

## Option 1: Professional Shredding Service

An outsourced shredding company either collects your documents (off-site shredding) or brings a truck to shred on your premises (mobile/on-site shredding), then provides a certificate of destruction.

**Pros:**
- No equipment to manage or maintain
- Handles very large, irregular volumes in one visit
- Formal chain-of-custody documentation for every batch

**Cons:**
- Documents often sit in a locked bin between collections — a window of risk
- Priced per visit or per bin, which adds up for regular use
- Scheduling is on the provider's calendar, not yours

## Option 2: Rented Office Shredder

A shredder delivered to your office, available whenever you need it, with maintenance included in the rental fee.

**Pros:**
- Destroy documents immediately — no waiting for a collection date
- Fixed monthly cost from AED 150, all-inclusive of maintenance and repairs
- No documents sitting in a bin waiting to be collected

**Cons:**
- Someone on your team has to operate it
- Not designed for extremely high daily volumes (100+ pages/day needs a departmental or heavy-duty model)

## When a Professional Shredding Service Still Makes Sense

- Very high daily volumes (warehouse or archive clear-outs measured in tonnes, not pages)
- Regulatory requirements for a certified destruction audit trail on every single batch
- No staff time available to operate a machine, even occasionally

## FAQ

**Q: Is professional shredding more secure than an office shredder?**
A: Both meet UAE PDPL requirements when done to DIN P-4 or higher. The main security difference is timing — an on-site shredder destroys documents immediately, while outsourced services involve a storage window between collection points. Neither is inherently "more secure" if handled correctly.

**Q: Can I combine both — rent a shredder day-to-day and use a service for annual clear-outs?**
A: Yes, and many UAE offices do exactly this — a rented shredder for routine daily/weekly shredding, plus a short-term heavy-duty rental for the annual bulk clear-out. See our guide on [heavy-duty shredder rental for bulk clear-outs](/blogs/heavy-duty-shredder-rental-bulk-document-clearout-uae/).

**Q: Do I need a certificate of destruction either way?**
A: Sahara can provide destruction certificates for compliance audit documentation on request for rental customers, so this isn't exclusive to outsourced services.

**Q: What documents should never wait for a collection date?**
A: Anything with personal identifiers — ID copies, payroll data, medical records, financial statements. See our checklist of [what documents to shred](/blogs/what-documents-to-shred-uae-office-checklist/).
