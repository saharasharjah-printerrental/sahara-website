## Step 1: Match Sheet Capacity to Team Size

| Team size | Recommended sheets/pass | Example model |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 people, occasional use | 8–10 sheets/pass | Fellowes Powershred LX65 |
| 4–10 people, daily shared use | 15–20 sheets/pass | Mid-range departmental cross-cut |
| 10+ people, high-volume department | 24+ sheets/pass | Fellowes Powershred 325Ci |

A shredder undersized for its team jams constantly and burns out faster — the single most common buying mistake in shared UAE offices.

## Step 2: Choose the Security Level

DIN 66399 defines the particle size a shredder cuts documents into. For most UAE office documents, **DIN P-4 cross-cut** is the accepted minimum for compliance with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). Legal, HR, and financial records handling highly sensitive data should step up to micro-cut (P-5 or higher).

## Step 3: Check Bin Capacity and Duty Cycle

A small bin means constant emptying; an undersized duty cycle (continuous run time before a cool-down period) means downtime in a busy office. Departmental machines like the Fellowes 325Ci are built for continuous multi-user runs — personal machines like the LX65 are not.
