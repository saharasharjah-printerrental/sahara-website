## Skip / Do Not Copy From Competitors

Most high-DA rows in the export are not real opportunities. They include random hosted pages (`vercel.app`, `pages.dev`, `web.app`, `hostingersite.com`), irrelevant university/blog pages (`udel.edu`, `gsu.edu`, `googleblog.com`), personal/social profiles with no business relevance (`ello.co`, `huggingface.co`), typo/spam directories (`clicktoselldirectoy.com`, `busniessdirectory.com`), and generic SEO/link directories (`rankwebdirectory.com`, `seohelperdirectory.com`, `advancedseodirectory.com`, `trafficdirectory.org`). These may inflate competitor backlink counts, but copying them would add risk without useful local authority.

Also skip `printersonrental.com` and `rentalsz.com` as direct competitor/lead-gen assets, not neutral citation platforms.


## Account / Verification Dependencies

- Hashnode, APSense, The Dots, ProvenExpert, Storeboard posting, and GetListedUAE require account login or account creation. The browser agent can fill/publish after the owner logs in, but should not invent credentials.

- Localsearch/DubaiLocal may require paid or managed listing processes; verify cost and edit control before submitting Sahara data.

- For review platforms, invite only real customers. Do not fabricate reviews for link acquisition.
