# Sahara 30-Day Backlink Campaign - September 2026

This is the active execution pack for building high-quality inbound links without paid placements. It uses relationship requests, useful content, and clean UAE citations. Do not count a link as acquired until the public source URL is checked and logged.

## Campaign Rules

- Fees: AED 0 placement budget.
- Primary targets: supplier/partner pages, customer deployment stories, relevant UAE business citations, and trade/editorial resources.
- Do not request paid ranking links, link exchanges, PBN placements, spam directories, or comment links.
- Do not require a followed link in outreach. Ask for a direct useful listing or citation; record the publisher's final link attributes during verification.
- Customer names, testimonials, project sizes, photos, and performance numbers require customer permission or written evidence.
- Publisher chooses link attributes. Record `nofollow`, `sponsored`, redirects, or JavaScript-only links honestly.

## Week-by-Week Execution

### Week 1 - Qualification and Evidence

- Confirm duplicates before any free listing submission.
- Gather proof for relationship asks: Bravo authorization scope, Canon/Kyocera dealer evidence, PaperCut eligibility, and customer permission.
- Confirm the rental guide page destination, canonical URL, and business details before outreach.
- Move targets that require account creation to "Owner action needed" rather than attempting account creation on the user's behalf.

### Week 2 - Relationship Requests

Send after owner approval:

1. Bravo Global partner listing request.
2. PaperCut reseller application enquiry.
3. Canon ME dealer locator request, only with dealer proof.
4. Kyocera/GCG sub-dealer listing request, only with dealer proof.
5. Five customer/partner intro requests using the templates in `docs/seo/outreach-drafts/relationship-link-requests.md`.

### Week 3 - Useful Resource Outreach

- Publish or stage the completed cost guide: `docs/seo/blog-drafts/printer-rental-vs-buying-uae-2026-cost-breakdown.md`.
- Use the calculator as the practical destination: `https://www.saharaprinter.com/rental-calculator/`.
- Pitch five relevant publishers or partner blogs with a specific editor-friendly angle, not a generic guest-post request.

### Week 4 - Listings and Follow-Up

- Prepare up to five free listing submissions from the tracker only after checking for duplicates.
- Follow up once after seven business days on relationship requests.
- Verify live URLs and update status only from public evidence.
