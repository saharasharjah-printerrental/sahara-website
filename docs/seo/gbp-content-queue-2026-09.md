## Review response templates

GBP tracks response rate and average response time as visible signals — respond within
a few days, every time, positive or negative. Personalize the bracketed part; don't
paste verbatim with brackets left in.

**5-star, no specifics mentioned:**
> Thank you [Name] for the kind review! We're glad the team could help — don't hesitate to reach out again for your next printer, copier, or repair need.

**5-star, mentions repair:**
> Thank you [Name]! Fast, reliable repair is exactly what we aim for — glad our technician got you back up and running quickly. We're here 4-hour response, Dubai-wide, whenever you need us again.

**5-star, mentions rental/AMC:**
> Thanks so much [Name] — glad the rental/AMC plan is working well for [Company]. Reach out anytime if you need to add machines or adjust the plan as you grow.

**4-star or below, generic:**
> Thank you for the feedback, [Name] — we'd like to understand what fell short of a great experience. Please call us at +971 50 382 3969 or email info@saharaprinter.com so we can make it right.

**1-2 star:** Do not use a template — read the specific complaint and respond to the actual issue, briefly and without defensiveness, then take the resolution offline (phone/email). A generic-sounding response to a serious complaint reads worse than no response.
