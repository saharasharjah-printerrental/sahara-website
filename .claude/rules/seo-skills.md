## AEO Answer Block Pattern
Every service page hero needs:
- Gold-bordered "AEO Answer Box"
- Label: "What is [X] in UAE?"
- 2-3 sentence direct answer with AED prices, response times
- Optimized for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity

## Content Structure for AI Citation
1. Definition block (first 200 words)
2. Numbered steps (how-to)
3. Comparison tables
4. FAQ block (Q&A pairs)
5. Statistics with attribution
